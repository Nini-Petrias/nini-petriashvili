About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Political and Social Sciences at the European University Institute in Florence. My research focuses on two main areas. First, I study political belief updating in the context of human–AI interaction, with particular attention to how different types of information environments (e.g., data voids) shape these processes. Second, I examine national identity formation and institutional development in post-Soviet countries.

Before joining the EUI, I obtained master's degrees from the University of Mannheim (2023) and Central European University (2020). In 2026, I was a visiting student at the Wilf Family Department of Politics at New York University and at NYU's Center for Social Media, AI, and Politics (CSMaP).
